## 14. Coon & Friends

Featured bosses: Hank Hill, Meg, Butters, Mint-Berry Crunch.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Butters is holding a superhero recruitment cookout. Hank supervises the grill, Mint-Berry Crunch supplies refreshments, and Meg arrived because the invitation specifically said she was invited.

Panel 1 — Butters stands behind a recruitment table. Hank guards a propane grill. Mint-Berry Crunch hovers beside a punch bowl. Meg clutches her invitation.
Butters: “Welcome to superhero recruitment!”
Meg: “Somebody invited me?”
Butters: “Twice. You kept checking.”

Panel 2 — Hank inspects a pink flamingo-shaped bag beside the grill. Its label reads DR. T’S COOKOUT KIT.
Hank Hill: “Free cookout kit? I don't trust a bird with no BTU rating.”

Panel 3 — Butters erects the flamingo centerpiece. Its eyes glow. Hank, Meg, and Mint-Berry Crunch turn toward it.
Butters: “Oh, neat! Mind control!”
Meg: “Will it make them remember my name?”

Panel 4 — Pink spirals spread. Hank calls the C-Team while pointing at the contents of the bag.
Hank Hill: “We need the C-Team.”
Butters: “For the mind control?”
Hank Hill: “Charcoal. This is how civilization ends.”
