## 20. NYC Rooftop

Featured bosses: Spidey (Fantastic 4), Green Goblin, Shredder, Michelangelo.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Shredder has called a rooftop meeting about noise complaints. Spidey represents the residents, Green Goblin disputes the parking rules, and Michelangelo came because “meeting” sounded close enough to “pizza.”

Panel 1 — Shredder stands beside a ROOFTOP RULES board. Spidey hangs upside down. Green Goblin’s glider occupies three parking spaces. Michelangelo carries an empty pizza box.
Shredder: “There will be order on this rooftop.”
Michelangelo: “Large. Extra cheese.”

Panel 2 — Shredder points at the empty box. Spidey gestures toward the glider.
Spidey: “The glider's in three parking spaces.”
Michelangelo: “And none of them deliver.”

Panel 3 — Shredder unveils a pink flamingo weather vane marked DR. T’S NEIGHBORHOOD IMPROVEMENT. It spins, then fixes its glowing eyes on all four.
Shredder: “Dr. T's bird will enforce obedience.”
Spidey: “You outsourced being threatening?”

Panel 4 — Spidey sends a C-Team distress message as their eyes spiral. Michelangelo looks delighted by the accidental group formation.
Michelangelo: “Dude, we're a team now!”
Shredder: “This is a hostage situation.”
Shredder: “So... team-building.”
