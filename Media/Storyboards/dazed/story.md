## 13. Wherever Psyduck Lives

Featured bosses: Granny, Wilma, Psyduck, Charizard.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Granny and Wilma are trying to treat Psyduck’s headache. Charizard volunteered to heat the compresses and has misunderstood the temperature requirements.

Panel 1 — Psyduck sits on a log holding its head. Granny prepares a cloth. Wilma holds a bucket. Charizard waits eagerly beside them.
Granny: “He needs a cold compress.”
Wilma: “So you called the dragon.”
Granny: “He answered first.”

Panel 2 — Charizard gently breathes on the cloth. It immediately catches fire.
Wilma: “Warm the cloth, dear.”
Granny: “It now qualifies as weather.”

Panel 3 — Wilma activates a pink flamingo labeled DR. T’S HEADACHE RELIEF. Its eyes rotate in hypnotic circles. Everyone looks up.
Wilma: “Dr. T's bird promises headache relief.”
Granny: “Does it take insurance?”

Panel 4 — Their eyes spiral. Granny uses a phone while Psyduck watches the burning cloth drift away.
Wilma: “He's lost his mind!”
Granny: “Bless him. First quiet afternoon he's had.”
