## 8. Wonka Factory

Featured bosses: Mrs. Butterworth, Ms. Pac-Man, Oompa Loompa, Red M&M.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: The factory is conducting a product inspection. Mrs. Butterworth tests coatings, Ms. Pac-Man tests consumption, Oompa Loompa guides the tour, and Red attends to make sure nobody mistakes him for inventory.

Panel 1 — Oompa Loompa leads the group along a conveyor. Mrs. Butterworth carries a syrup sample. Ms. Pac-Man eagerly watches the candy. Red wears a huge VISITOR badge.
Red M&M: “I'm a visiting executive.”
Oompa Loompa: “Then stop getting sorted by color.”

Panel 2 — Ms. Pac-Man follows Red with her eyes. Red puts on a second badge reading NOT A SAMPLE.
Red M&M: “Stop looking at me like lunch!”
Ms. Pac-Man: “Dessert.”

Panel 3 — Mrs. Butterworth unveils a pink flamingo awarded by DR. T’S QUALITY CONTROL. It lights up. Ms. Pac-Man stops chewing; the others stare.
Mrs. Butterworth: “Dr. T's seal of approval. Such a nice gentleman.”
Red M&M: “You say that about anybody with a lid.”

Panel 4 — Red sends a trembling message to the C-Team. The conveyor pulls his visitor badge toward a packaging machine.
Red M&M: “I'm being gift-wrapped!”
Oompa Loompa: “You chose the deluxe tour.”
Red M&M: “I WANTED A LANYARD!”
