## 24. Office Hours

Featured bosses: Deadpool & Wolverine, Joker + Batmobile, Dr. Trendowski, Chuck Norris.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Dr. T has called his final enforcer orientation. Deadpool brought Wolverine to dispute the unpaid contract, Joker drove the Batmobile straight into the office, and Chuck Norris came to explain why his name does not belong on a sign-in sheet.

Panel 1 — Dr. T stands at his desk. Deadpool and Wolverine share one visitor badge. The Batmobile is wedged through the doorway with Joker grinning behind the wheel. Chuck stands beside an untouched sign-in sheet.
Dr. T: “Welcome to final enforcer orientation.”
Deadpool: “One badge for two people? Even our benefits are a duo.”

Panel 2 — Wolverine drops a pink flamingo on the desk. Behind Dr. T sits a control panel labeled HYPNOSIS: RANDOM TARGETS.
Wolverine: “You sent those birds everywhere?”
Dr. T: “Random selection. Very fair.”
Deadpool: “Your henchmen have better hiring odds than your students.”

Panel 3 — Dr. T activates the remaining flamingos. Their eyes glow toward the entire office—including his desk. Pink spirals appear in the visitors’ eyes and his own.
Dr. T: “Now everyone obeys me.”
Chuck Norris: “You're in the beam, genius.”

Panel 4 — Hypnotized Dr. T writes MANDATORY GROUP PROJECT. Deadpool recoils; Wolverine points toward the door. Joker’s grin falters and Chuck snaps the sign-in pencil. A C-Team emergency button flashes.
Dr. T: “One grade for the whole group.”
Deadpool: “I preferred the mind control.”
Wolverine: “I'm dropping this class.”
