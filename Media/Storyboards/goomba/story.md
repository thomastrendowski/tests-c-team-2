## 5. Goomba Gauntlet

Featured bosses: Samus, Ganon, Larry, Princess Peach.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Peach has hired Samus to inspect suspicious activity around the castle. Larry is the cheapest available contractor. Ganon insists the suspicious activity is his intellectual property.

Panel 1 — Peach presents a repair list. Larry carries a toolbox held together with tape. Samus scans the area. Ganon looms beside a cracked wall.
Peach: “Larry, can you remove the evil?”
Larry: “Is it load-bearing?”

Panel 2 — Ganon points proudly at a patch of ominous purple magic. Samus aims her scanner at it.
Ganon: “That is ancient darkness!”
Larry: “So... asbestos pricing.”

Panel 3 — Larry installs a pink flamingo marked DR. T’S BUDGET SECURITY SYSTEM. Its glowing eyes sweep across the inspection party.
Larry: “The bird was free. Saved you a fortune.”
Peach: “It has a warranty?”
Larry: “It has a sticker.”

Panel 4 — Pink spirals appear. Peach reaches for her emergency communicator while Samus scans the flamingo’s tiny warranty label.
Peach: “I want a refund!”
Larry: “On the free bird?”
Ganon: “I respect this contractor.”
