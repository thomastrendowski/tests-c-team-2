## 17. Haunted Hollow

Featured bosses: Slimer, Abraham Lincoln: Vampire Slayer, Scooby, Shaggy + Mystery Machine.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Shaggy and Scooby are inspecting a supposedly haunted property. Lincoln came to eliminate vampires, while Slimer insists he is the current tenant and has already paid the snack deposit.

Panel 1 — The Mystery Machine sits outside a creaking house. Lincoln grips his axe. Scooby hides behind Shaggy. Slimer holds an empty snack wrapper.
Shaggy: “Like, the listing said 'full of character.'”
Lincoln: “So did the last house. It had nine vampires.”

Panel 2 — Slimer floats beside a lease. The deposit section reads ONE REFRIGERATOR.
Shaggy: “You ate the fridge?”
Slimer: “Kept the light. Midnight snack.”

Panel 3 — Lincoln uncovers a pink flamingo left on the porch, tagged DR. T’S SPIRITUAL CLEANSING. Its eyes glow across the group.
Lincoln: “Dr. T's spiritual cleansing.”
Shaggy: “Like, does it also do carpets?”

Panel 4 — Shaggy reaches into the van for the emergency radio. Everyone begins developing spiral eyes.
Shaggy: “Scoob, the lawn ornament's got us!”
Lincoln: “I freed a nation. I will not be defeated by patio decor.”
