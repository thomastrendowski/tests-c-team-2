## 16. Minion’s Pizza Panic

Featured bosses: Minion, Iron Man, Sketchy Hot Dog, Colonel Sanders.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Minion is opening a pizza restaurant. Iron Man is considering an investment, Colonel Sanders wants a franchise deal, and Sketchy Hot Dog claims he is the restaurant’s business consultant.

Panel 1 — Minion presents a banana-covered pizza. Iron Man scans it. Colonel Sanders studies a contract. Sketchy Hot Dog sits in a chair labeled CONSULTANT.
Iron Man: “Your business plan is a banana.”
Minion: “Banana!”
Colonel Sanders: “At least he brought the original.”

Panel 2 — Colonel Sanders checks the consultant’s paperwork. Sketchy Hot Dog has signed it with mustard.
Colonel Sanders: “You signed the contract in mustard.”
Sketchy Hot Dog: “My lawyer is relish.”

Panel 3 — Minion switches on a pink flamingo promotional display from DR. T’S RESTAURANT STARTER KIT. Its eyes flash. The entire table stares.
Iron Man: “Dr. T's free display is broadcasting commands.”
Sketchy Hot Dog: “Finally. Management experience.”

Panel 4 — Iron Man’s wrist screen sends a C-Team alert. Everyone’s eyes spiral as Minion moves the consultant toward the pizza counter.
Colonel Sanders: “They're putting you on the menu!”
Sketchy Hot Dog: “Consulting fee: eight dollars.”
Minion: “With fries.”
Sketchy Hot Dog: “My benefits package!”
