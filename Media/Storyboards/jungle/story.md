## 11. ¡Vámonos! Jungle

Featured bosses: T-Rex, Wonder Woman, Dora, Lara Croft.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Dora is leading an expedition. Lara wants to inspect a newly discovered artifact. Wonder Woman handles safety, and the T-Rex has been hired to keep other dinosaurs away.

Panel 1 — Dora holds a map. Lara checks a stone pedestal. Wonder Woman keeps the T-Rex’s tail away from the supplies.
Dora: “Our expedition has security!”
Lara Croft: “Your security ate base camp.”

Panel 2 — The T-Rex wears a tiny SECURITY badge. It bites a wooden sign reading EXIT.
Lara Croft: “And the exit sign.”
Dora: “He's learning his words!”

Panel 3 — Dora opens a crate containing a pink flamingo tagged DR. T’S EXPLORATION GRANT. Its eyes glow as the group gathers around.
Dora: “A present from Dr. T!”
Lara Croft: “Wonderful. Our first artifact with customer support.”

Panel 4 — Dora’s emergency device sends a C-Team alert. Everyone’s eyes spiral. The T-Rex still holds the exit sign in its mouth.
Dora: “Can you find the emergency exit?”
Lara Croft: “Yes. It's currently being digested.”
