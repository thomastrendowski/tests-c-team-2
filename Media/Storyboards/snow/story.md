## 21. Island of Misfit Toys

Featured bosses: Skeletor, Uncle Eddie + RV, Hermey & Rudolph, Santa.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Santa is organizing holiday photographs. Eddie provided transport, Rudolph supplies lighting, Hermey offers smile preparation, and Skeletor wants a Christmas card that makes him look approachable.

Panel 1 — Santa sits in a photo chair. Eddie’s RV is parked directly behind the backdrop. Hermey checks Skeletor’s teeth while Rudolph lights the scene.
Hermey: “Excellent teeth.”
Skeletor: “I FLOSS WITH TERROR!”
Hermey: “I'll put 'occasionally.'”

Panel 2 — Santa pats his knee. Skeletor looks offended.
Santa: “On my lap for the photo.”
Skeletor: “I command armies!”
Santa: “Then command yourself to smile.”

Panel 3 — Eddie plugs in a pink flamingo decoration labeled DR. T’S FESTIVE LIGHTING. Its eyes glow brighter than Rudolph’s nose. Everyone turns toward it.
Hermey: “Eddie, that isn't approved lighting.”
Uncle Eddie: “It came with a doctor. That's medical-grade Christmas.”

Panel 4 — Pink spirals appear. Santa prepares the photo. Skeletor panics; Hermey holds the emergency phone. Eddie shows a newly printed holiday portrait attached to the RV behind them; Rudolph stays with Hermey.
Santa: “Who gets a copy?”
Skeletor: “NOT HE-MAN!”
Uncle Eddie: “Already printed it on the RV.”
