## 4. Tokoyo

Featured bosses: Megazord, Stay Puft, Godzilla, King Kong.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: The four have been called to a building-insurance inspection after repeatedly being blamed for damage to the same city block.

Panel 1 — Megazord holds an inspection clipboard. Godzilla stands beside a melted traffic light. King Kong discreetly hides a bent antenna. Stay Puft tries to fit through a tiny doorway.
Megazord: “This building passed inspection yesterday.”
Stay Puft: “We weren't here yesterday.”

Panel 2 — Megazord points to a form. Godzilla’s tail knocks over another streetlight. Kong quietly places the antenna behind Godzilla.
Megazord: “Stop generating evidence.”
Stay Puft: “I'm load-bearing now.”

Panel 3 — Stay Puft opens the insurer’s welcome hamper. Inside sits a pink flamingo labeled DR. T: RISK MANAGEMENT. Its glow reaches all four giants.
Stay Puft: “A tiny welcome gift! I can finally open something without zoning approval.”

Panel 4 — Their eyes spiral. Megazord’s clipboard displays an emergency message to the C-Team beside a rejected insurance claim.
Stay Puft: “Will insurance cover the bird?”
Megazord: “You selected the cheap plan.”
Stay Puft: “I'm thirty stories of marshmallow. EVERYTHING is a fire risk!”
