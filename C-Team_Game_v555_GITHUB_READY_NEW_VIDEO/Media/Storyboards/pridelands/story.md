## 1. The Pride Lands

Featured bosses: Donkey + Dragon, Goku, Nala, Mike Tyson.
Eligibility: Mike Tyson replaces Randall as this board’s boss and completion unlock under the creator’s new progression. Randall is now guaranteed at startup. These characters are local bosses, not established C-Team members.
Why they’re together: Nala is hosting a meeting about the savanna’s disappearing food. Goku is a suspect; Mike Tyson came to coach a savanna fitness class and found the buffet empty; Donkey has volunteered Dragon as an extremely intimidating character witness.

Nala: “A free gift from Dr. T. Finally, something nobody can eat.”
