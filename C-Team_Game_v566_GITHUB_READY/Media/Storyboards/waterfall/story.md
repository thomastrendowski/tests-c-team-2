## 3. Water “Fall”

Featured bosses: Ms. Frizzle, Olaf, Pocahontas, Mulan + Mushu.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Ms. Frizzle has arranged a waterfall field trip. Pocahontas guides it, Mulan supervises safety, and Olaf signed up because the brochure mentioned water.

Panel 1 — The group waits beside moving logs. Pocahontas points out the route. Mulan tightens Mushu’s tiny life jacket. Olaf proudly wears swimming goggles.
Ms. Frizzle: “Today: the water cycle!”
Olaf: “Can I audit? I'm becoming the handout.”

Panel 2 — Olaf’s feet are becoming puddles. Mushu studies a permission slip.
Mushu: “My permission slip says 'minor splashing.'”
Ms. Frizzle: “That's the font size.”

Panel 3 — A pink flamingo floats past on a log, tagged DR. T’S EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS. Ms. Frizzle lifts it for the group to inspect. Its eyes shine.
Ms. Frizzle: “Dr. T sent a teaching aid!”
Mushu: “Why does it need eye contact?”

Panel 4 — Everyone develops pink spirals. Mushu grabs the emergency phone attached to Ms. Frizzle’s bag.
Mushu: “I'm reporting this field trip!”
Ms. Frizzle: “To your parents?”
Mushu: “TO THE COAST GUARD!”
