## 2. Gotham City

Featured bosses: Pirate Batman, Mr. Freeze, Harley Quinn, Clayface.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Harley has organized a villain charity auction. Pirate Batman supplied stolen valuables, Mr. Freeze handles preservation, and Clayface has been mistaken for the display pedestal.

Panel 1 — Harley presides over an auction table. Pirate Batman guards a treasure chest. Mr. Freeze cools the room. Clayface holds up an expensive vase.
Harley: “All proceeds go to victims of crime.”
Pirate Batman: “Which victims?”
Harley: “Us. Taxes.”

Panel 2 — Harley sticks a price label directly on Clayface.
Clayface: “You put a price tag on me.”
Harley: “Relax. Nobody's bidding.”

Panel 3 — Harley unveils a pink flamingo marked SPECIAL DONATION: DR. T. Mr. Freeze spotlights it with an icy beam. Its eyes glow across all four bidders.
Harley: “Dr. T donated a bird! Finally, somebody with taste.”

Panel 4 — Everyone stiffens as the hypnosis spreads. Harley frantically taps C-TEAM on her phone. Clayface’s vase has become a pink bird.
Clayface: “I'm turning into merchandise!”
Harley: “Good. You're finally appreciating in value.”
