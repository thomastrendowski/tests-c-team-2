## 19. Smooth Criminal Cereal

Featured bosses: Michael Jackson, Dr. Robotnik, Lucky, Count Chocula.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Lucky and Count Chocula are recording a cereal commercial. Michael handles choreography, and Robotnik supplied the automated production equipment because nobody read the service agreement.

Panel 1 — Michael marks dance positions on the floor. Lucky holds a cereal box. Count Chocula worries over his bowl. Robotnik presents the automated factory.
Michael Jackson: “Count, hit your mark.”
Count Chocula: “No stakes on this set, correct?”

Panel 2 — Robotnik proudly gestures at a cereal machine while Lucky inspects its invoice.
Dr. Robotnik: “My machines replace the workforce!”
Lucky: “Can they replace the rent?”

Panel 3 — Lucky opens a box and finds a pink flamingo tagged DR. T’S FREE PRIZE. Its magical glow reaches everyone.
Lucky: “Look! Dr. T put a free prize in the box!”
Count Chocula: “Free. That's how they get you.”

Panel 4 — Pink light fills the factory. Robotnik reads a long subscription receipt labeled AUTOMATIC REFILLS. The others react; a screen displays C-TEAM: ALERT SENT.
Lucky: “It's controlling our minds!”
Dr. Robotnik: “Worse. I just read the subscription.”
Count Chocula: “What subscription?”
Dr. Robotnik: “AUTOMATIC REFILLS.”
