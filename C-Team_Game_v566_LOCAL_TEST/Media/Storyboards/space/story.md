## 9. Definitely Not Kansas

Featured bosses: Wednesday Addams, McFly + DeLorean, Wicked Witch, Snape.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: McFly has parked in the Witch’s garden. Snape came to inspect the unusual plants. Wednesday booked the garden because its opening section looked suitably colorless.

Panel 1 — The DeLorean sits in a flattened flowerbed. The Witch points angrily at it. Snape examines a crushed plant. Wednesday holds a holiday brochure.
Wicked Witch: “You flattened my poisonous flowers!”
McFly: “Okay, technically I improved the safety rating.”

Panel 2 — Wednesday compares the gray landscape with the colorful section ahead.
Wednesday: “The brochure promised bleak despair. This has landscaping.”

Panel 3 — The Witch installs a pink flamingo from DR. T’S GARDEN PROTECTION beside the car. Its eyes flare. Everyone looks directly at it.
Wicked Witch: “Dr. T says this bird removes unwanted visitors.”
McFly: “Does it validate parking first?”

Panel 4 — McFly’s dashboard flashes C-TEAM RESCUE REQUEST SENT. Wednesday’s eyes begin spiraling, but she looks almost pleased.
McFly: “The C-Team's coming!”
Wednesday: “Wonderful. They can leave a bad review under duress.”
