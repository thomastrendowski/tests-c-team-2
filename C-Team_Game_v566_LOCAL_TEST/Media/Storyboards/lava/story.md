## 23. Mordor

Featured bosses: Sauron, Daenerys, Darth Vader, Death Star.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Sauron is hosting an evil-leadership summit. Daenerys brought dragon-related demands, Vader brought a presentation, and the Death Star is parked outside because the venue advertised unlimited parking.

Panel 1 — Sauron stands at a dark conference table. Daenerys reviews a contract. Vader holds a presentation remote. The Death Star fills the window behind them.
Daenerys: “You said unlimited parking.”
Sauron: “Unlimited is the number of fees.”

Panel 2 — Vader looks out at the Death Star. Sauron slides him a parking invoice.
Darth Vader: “You charged by diameter?”
Sauron: “And marked it 'compact.'”

Panel 3 — Daenerys opens a complimentary pink flamingo centerpiece supplied by DR. T’S LEADERSHIP NETWORK. Its glow reaches the conference table and the Death Star outside.
Daenerys: “A free centerpiece from Dr. T.”
Darth Vader: “Even evil has overhead.”

Panel 4 — Vader’s communicator displays C-TEAM: URGENT. Pink spirals spread. Sauron stares helplessly at the bird.
Sauron: “I can see everything!”
Daenerys: “Then read the return policy.”
Darth Vader: “Store credit.”
Darth Vader: “NOOO!”
