## 7. PlayPlace

Featured bosses: Little Debbie, Tommy + Reptar, Grimace, Cookie Monster.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Grimace is testing a birthday-party package. Little Debbie supplies cakes, Cookie Monster evaluates snacks, and Tommy insists Reptar qualifies for the children’s discount.

Panel 1 — Grimace holds a party checklist. Little Debbie sets out cakes. Cookie Monster guards a tray. Tommy points to Reptar beside the height chart.
Grimace: “Children under this line eat free.”
Tommy: “Reptar's two.”
Grimace: “Two what?”

Panel 2 — Cookie Monster picks up a tablet displaying ACCEPT COOKIES?
Cookie Monster: “It says 'accept cookies.' Me finally found honest advertising.”

Panel 3 — Little Debbie activates a pink flamingo party centerpiece labeled DR. T’S PARTY FAVOR. Its eyes light up. Everyone stops mid-snack.
Grimace: “Dr. T's party favor has a mind-control setting.”
Tommy: “Can it make Reptar take a nap?”

Panel 4 — Grimace tries to send a C-Team rescue request. The tablet has a large bite missing. A pink magical haze and spiral pupils show the group falling under the flamingo’s control.
Grimace: “You ate the emergency tablet!”
Cookie Monster: “Me accepted cookies.”
Grimace: “Those were settings!”
Cookie Monster: “Needed salt.”
