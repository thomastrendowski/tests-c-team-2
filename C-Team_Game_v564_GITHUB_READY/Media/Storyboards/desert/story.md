## 12. Shaq Shaqti’s Sand Trap

Featured bosses: Browns QBs 99-26, Night King, Shaq(dis), Michael Scott.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Michael booked a sports leadership retreat. Shaq is the keynote speaker, the Browns quarterbacks are practicing teamwork, and the Night King was advertised as complimentary air conditioning.

Panel 1 — Michael stands beneath a crooked LEADERSHIP RETREAT banner. Shaq towers over the chairs. The quarterbacks crowd one bench. The Night King cools a single bottle of water.
Michael Scott: “Leadership begins outside your comfort zone.”
Shaq: “Michael, this is outside civilization.”

Panel 2 — A quarterback throws a football past the meeting. Michael points proudly at the Night King.
Michael Scott: “The Night King supplies free air conditioning.”
Shaq: “He froze the bathroom.”
Michael Scott: “So it never needs cleaning.”

Panel 3 — Michael presents a pink flamingo trophy donated by DR. T’S EXECUTIVE DEVELOPMENT. Its eyes illuminate the entire gathering.
Michael Scott: “Dr. T donated our team-building exercise!”
Shaq: “You outsourced teamwork?”

Panel 4 — Everyone begins falling into trance. Michael tries to call the C-Team while sinking into the sand.
Shaq: “Call the C-Team.”
Michael Scott: “I can't. I'm being promoted.”
Shaq: “To what?”
Michael Scott: “Lawn ornament.”
