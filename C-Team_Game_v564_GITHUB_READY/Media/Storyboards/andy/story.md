## 15. Andy’s Room

Featured bosses: Little Green Man, Ahsoka Tano, Buzz, Woody.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Woody called a toy-safety meeting. Buzz is enforcing regulations, Ahsoka is demonstrating safe handling of dangerous equipment, and Little Green Man joined because he thought the desk lamp was a claw.

Panel 1 — Woody presides over a meeting on the floor. Buzz holds a checklist. Ahsoka carefully places her weapons out of reach. Little Green Man gazes upward at the lamp.
Woody: “Stop worshipping the lamp.”
Little Green Man: “The lamp hears everything.”
Woody: “It's unplugged.”

Panel 2 — Buzz holds up a pink flamingo tagged DR. T’S APPROVED SAFETY MARKER.
Buzz: “Certified safe by Dr. T.”
Woody: “Certified where?”
Buzz: “On this very convincing tag.”

Panel 3 — The flamingo’s eyes glow. Little Green Man reaches toward it; the other three follow its light.
Little Green Man: “The bird has chosen us!”
Woody: “Stop joining things!”

Panel 4 — Woody presses an emergency communicator as everyone’s eyes spiral.
Woody: “We're being recalled.”
Buzz: “For what?”
Ahsoka: “Apparently the warning labels were accurate.”
