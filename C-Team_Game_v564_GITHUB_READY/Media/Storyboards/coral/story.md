## 10. Bikini Bottom

Featured bosses: The Rock, Amy Rose, Patrick, Zoidberg.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: The Rock is running a fitness session. Amy brought her hammer for strength training, Patrick brought a rock because he misunderstood the instructor’s name, and Zoidberg appointed himself the medical staff.

Panel 1 — The Rock stands beside exercise equipment. Amy carries her hammer. Patrick proudly presents a small stone. Zoidberg wears a medical bag backward.
The Rock: “Show me what you're working with.”
Patrick: “Your cousin. He's between jobs.”

Panel 2 — Zoidberg checks Patrick’s pulse by pinching his wrist.
Patrick: “Why are you pinching me?”
Zoidberg: “Checking circulation. Mine. It's terrible.”

Panel 3 — Amy sets up a pink flamingo marked DR. T’S BALANCE TRAINER. Everyone attempts its one-legged pose. Its eyes begin glowing.
The Rock: “Focus on the bird.”
Patrick: “Is this leg day or beak day?”

Panel 4 — Pink spirals spread. Patrick struggles to dial the C-Team while Zoidberg studies an empty medical form.
Patrick: “It stole my brain!”
Zoidberg: “Describe it.”
Patrick: “Never used. Original packaging.”
