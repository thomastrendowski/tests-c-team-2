## 18. Sentinel Factory

Featured bosses: Professor Timothy, Optimus Prime, Mystique, Storm.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Professor Timothy is conducting employee orientation. Optimus has been mistaken for factory equipment, Storm handles ventilation, and Mystique has arrived under four different employee identities.

Optimus Prime: “I am not a forklift.”
Professor Timothy: “Then why did you accept the parking permit?”

Panel 2 — Mystique displays four badges, all with different photographs.
Mystique: “Four identities. Four salaries.”
Professor Timothy: “Four mandatory orientations.”

Panel 3 — Timothy activates a pink flamingo labeled DR. T’S STAFF ENGAGEMENT SYSTEM. Its eyes glow. All four look toward it.
Professor Timothy: “Dr. T's engagement system. Please look directly at management.”
Mystique: “Which management? I'm three of them.”

Panel 4 — Pink spirals spread. Mystique taps a C-Team rescue request while juggling her badges.
Mystique: “I'm taking four sick days.”
Professor Timothy: “One employee per body.”
Storm: “Suddenly you believe in teamwork.”
