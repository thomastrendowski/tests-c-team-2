## 22. Detention on Evergreen Terrace

Featured bosses: Groundskeeper Willie, Ralph Wiggum, Maggie, Barbarian Homer + Mage Marge.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: The family is attending a school garden volunteer day. Willie supervises, Ralph is the student helper, Maggie rides along, and Homer thought “planting beds” meant an opportunity to nap.

Panel 1 — Willie angrily points at an unfinished garden. Ralph holds crayons instead of gardening tools. Maggie watches from her stroller. Homer lies in a flowerbed while Marge stands over him.
Marge: “Homer, help with the garden.”
Homer: “I'm composting slowly.”

Panel 2 — Ralph places a pink flamingo marked DONATED BY DR. T beside the garden.
Ralph: “My shovel has a face!”
Marge: “That's a flamingo, Ralph.”
Ralph: “Then my shovel is undercover.”

Panel 3 — The flamingo’s eyes light up. Everyone turns toward it. Willie’s rake drops; Maggie’s pacifier freezes mid-bob.
Marge: “Homer, stop staring at the bird.”
Homer: “It hasn't asked me to do a single chore.”

Panel 4 — Their eyes spiral. Ralph has scribbled C-TEAM HELP across Willie’s phone screen.
Marge: “That thing controls your mind!”
Homer: “Finally. Someone else can remember the passwords.”
