## 6. Partly Cloudy Painting

Featured bosses: Share Bear, Frodo + Gimli, Bob Ross, Eeyore.
Eligibility: All four entries are this board’s assigned bosses, appearing as locals before gameplay. They are not established C-Team members; playable and Morph-Up unlock timing is not used.
Why they’re together: Bob is teaching a painting class. Share Bear brought supplies, Frodo and Gimli are posing for a portrait, and Eeyore has been recruited as the rain-cloud model.

Panel 1 — Bob paints at an easel. Share Bear distributes brushes. Frodo sits patiently while Gimli struggles to remain still. Eeyore stands beneath a little rain cloud.
Bob Ross: “No mistakes. Just happy accidents.”
Gimli: “Then my axe has had an excellent career.”

Panel 2 — Share Bear places a pink flamingo among the still-life objects. Its tag reads ART SUPPLIES COURTESY OF DR. T.
Share Bear: “Everyone gets to share!”
Eeyore: “Take my cloud. It's fully paid off.”

Panel 3 — Bob studies the bird closely. Its eyes glow. The group’s painted portraits acquire pink spiral eyes at the same moment their real eyes do.
Bob Ross: “Let's give Dr. T's little bird a friend.”
Eeyore: “Don't. It recruits.”

Panel 4 — Share Bear’s phone displays C-TEAM: HELP. Bob accidentally paints bars across his landscape.
Bob Ross: “We'll paint over the problem.”
Eeyore: “Lovely. Kidnapped in watercolor.”
