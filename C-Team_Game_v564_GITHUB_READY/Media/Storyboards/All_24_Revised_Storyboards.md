# The C-Team — Revised Boss-Origin Comics

Source: C-Team_Game_v542_HORIZONTAL_WATERFALL, with the creator’s override: Randall is a starter; Mike Tyson replaces Randall as a Pride Lands boss and completion unlock. Awesomo is guaranteed at startup.

Proposed additions: Dr. T sends free flamingos to gatherings across the 24 worlds. The gifts take control during each comic’s final two panels. The C-Team receives emergency alerts through phones and screens; the bosses have not yet been rescued. Free gifts and unread terms escalate toward Office Hours, where Dr. T also falls under his own hypnosis and assigns one group grade. Completing Office Hours ends the operation during gameplay.

The scenes follow the actual board order. Only each board’s local boss cast appears. No fixed player character, optional favorite, or previously unlocked crew is assumed. Duo partners and vehicles stay together. No narration boxes.
