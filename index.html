How to Play

START A RUN
Choose a lecture or quiz for Content Review, or choose Play Without Questions. Select your characters, choose a Morph Up, then pick a level. Character Profiles let you view stats and practice powers.
CONTROLS
P1: ← / → move, P jump, O or F Action/Power, I switch characters.
P2: A / D move, W or C jump, S or B SWAP characters, V Action/Power. Click a SWAP button to control your other selected character. Morph Ups can double jump; some also dash, fly, or swap partners.
GOAL AND GAME MODES
Collect all pink flamingos and defeat both pairs of bosses. In Content Review, flamingos open questions from the selected bank; read the feedback and continue. Play Without Questions skips the questions and uses character health. Switch characters when you need your teammate. Correct answers and pickups add score; taking hits can cost score or health, depending on the mode.
MORPH UPS AND POWERS
Choose one of the 30 Morph Ups on the selection screen. Hit a ? box to release an emoji Morph Up pickup, then collect it to transform. Use Action/Power for that form's ability. Morph Ups absorb two hits without losing score or normal HP: the first removes one shield point; the second returns you to your original character. Both hits are protected, with brief invulnerability afterward. Stun and pull abilities keep enemies alive; damaging powers can defeat them. Recharge stars: 5 = 2.5s, 4 = 4.5s, 3 = 6.5s, 2 = 8.5s, 1 = 11s. Some powers allow only one projectile at a time. Six Morph Ups start available; the others are assigned to the 24 boards. The current test build keeps profile practice available.
FOUR BOSS TYPES ON EVERY BOARD
Shield: 3 HP. Shield on for 1 second, off for 3. Stomp or use a damaging power while it is off.
Double health: 6 successful hits.
Jumping/flying: 3 HP with brief airtime; attack in the air or after landing. Flying bosses fly for 2 seconds, then stay low for 4.
TNT: Normal attacks do no damage. Stand near a box and press Action to arm it. Move away during the 2-second fuse. Three explosions defeat the boss. Boss labels show their type and remaining HP. In Mordor, use the moving platform to reach the Death Star and the stationary platform to avoid lava.
FINISH A LEVEL
After collecting every flamingo and defeating all four bosses, walk up to the exit and press Action/Power. In Goomba Gauntlet, stand on the pipe first. The same Action button uses your power, arms nearby TNT, or activates a ready exit, depending on where you are.
